Overview.

I'm a Lead Software Engineer with 5+ years of experience designing and building full-stack applications, distributed systems, and data-intensive platforms across marketing technology, healthcare, and SaaS. My experience spans frontend engineering, backend architecture, cloud infrastructure, and application security. I enjoy tackling complex engineering problems, improving existing systems, and building software that performs reliably at scale.

Currently at Epsilon, I work on the architecture and development of the Digital Customer Data Platform (DCDP), focusing on enterprise authorization and data security. I build Python-based services, Angular and TypeScript applications, and access-control solutions using Okta, Open Policy Agent (OPA), and Databricks Unity Catalog. My work also involves optimizing Kafka-driven event processing, automating permission auditing, and improving reliability across distributed services and cloud environments.

Previously at Paradigm Healthcare Services, I helped develop the Student Health Network (SHN), an EHR platform supporting hundreds of California school districts and over $100M in annual Medicaid reimbursements. I engineered healthcare API integrations for 8+ partners, developed analytics dashboards that improved billing efficiency by 25% and helped recover $1M+ in previously denied claims, and built a fully offline AI search system using LangChain, FAISS, and Mistral. I also tackled production performance issues, resolving memory leaks and optimizing infrastructure to reduce costs by 40%.

At DataChat, I developed conversational analytics capabilities with 100+ domain-aware commands, helping business users explore data and generate visualizations through natural language. I also built revenue-generating SaaS features, worked on database connectivity across Snowflake, BigQuery, and Databricks, and automated Kubernetes infrastructure provisioning with Terraform, reducing environment setup from weeks to hours.

Beyond writing code, I lead technical design discussions, review architecture and code, and work with engineering teams to troubleshoot production issues. I like staying close to the implementation while taking responsibility for the bigger picture, whether that's designing a new system, improving performance, or making an existing platform more secure and reliable.
